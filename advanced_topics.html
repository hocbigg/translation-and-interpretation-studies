Advanced Topics in Translation and Interpretation Studies

This curriculum branches out from the Core Curriculum into five specialized tracks representing distinct subdisciplines and high-level professional pathways. Learners should select one or two tracks aligned with their specific career objectives or research interests rather than attempting to complete all tracks sequentially.

  • Specialized Domain Translation - Advanced translation practice in legal, medical, and scientific/technical domains, emphasizing specialized terminology, statutory conventions, and conceptual rigor.
  • Literary Translation - The aesthetics, hermeneutics, and craft of translating fiction, poetry, and creative prose, including voice modulation and cultural transfer.
  • Audiovisual Translation and Localization - Technical constraints and semiotic strategies governing subtitling, media accessibility, and video game transcreation.
  • Advanced Interpreting - High-stakes simultaneous conference interpreting and complex community-based dialogue interpreting across institutional settings.
  • Translation Technology and MT Post-Editing - Computational architectures of neural machine translation, enterprise localization pipelines, corpus linguistics, and post-editing standards.

Specialized Domain Translation

This track develops advanced translation competence in high-liability technical domains where terminological precision and genre compliance are decisive.

Legal Translation: Legal Translation Explained (Enrique Alcaraz & Brian Hughes) - A systematic guide to common law and civil law terminology, statutory drafting conventions, and translational strategies for binding legal instruments.

Medical Translation: Medical Translation Step by Step (Vicent Montalt & Maria González-Davies) - A task-based methodology covering medical communication genres, clinical trial protocols, regulatory documentation, and pharmacology.

Medical Terminology Foundation: Medical Terminology Course (Des Moines University) - A comprehensive, self-paced open course detailing root words, prefixes, and suffixes across anatomical systems, establishing the physiological grounding required for medical translation.

Scientific and Technical Translation: Scientific and Technical Translation Explained (Jody Byrne) - An open-access guide focusing on technical communication theory, user documentation ergonomics, passive construction handling, and patent translation strategies.

Literary Translation

This track examines the interpretive, philosophical, and stylistic challenges of rendering creative voice, cultural resonance, and poetic form across languages.

Translation Poetics and Visibility: The Translator's Invisibility: A History of Translation (Lawrence Venuti) - A foundational critical history analyzing the cultural consequences of ethnocentric fluency, articulating the seminal dichotomy between domesticating and foreignizing translation.

Philosophy and Hermeneutics: After Babel: Aspects of Language and Translation (George Steiner) - A classic, borrowable treatise exploring the hermeneutic motion, linguistic relativity, the limits of untranslatability, and the cognitive ontology of interlingual transfer.

Literary Craft and Stylistics: Literary Translation: A Practical Guide (Clifford E. Landers) - A pragmatic handbook addressing the micro-decisions of translating prose fiction, including character idiolects, puns, regional dialects, and publishing relationships.

Contemporary Practice and Masterclasses: The Sebald Lectures on Literary Translation (British Centre for Literary Translation) - An open lecture series featuring masterclasses by renowned literary translators and authors on creative ethics, voice, and the lived art of literary mediation.

Audiovisual Translation and Localization

This track covers screen translation modalities, accessibility requirements, and interactive media adaptation governed by multimodal and temporal constraints.

Subtitling Concepts and Technical Constraints: Subtitling: Concepts and Practices (Jorge Díaz Cintas & Aline Remael) - The standard modern handbook detailing spotting, spatial and temporal constraints, semiotic cohesion, condensation strategies, and humor adaptation.

Industry Subtitling Standards: Netflix Timed Text Style Guide: General Requirements (Netflix Partner Help Center) - An authoritative industry standard detailing character limits, reading speeds, event duration, line-break rules, and audio synchronization parameters.

Media Accessibility and Audio Description: ADLAB PRO Course Materials (ADLAB PRO Consortium) - A free, modular European training curriculum with video modules, transcripts, and exercises for crafting audio descriptions for blind and visually impaired audiences.

Video Game Localization: Game Localization: Translating for the Global Digital Entertainment Industry (Minako O'Hagan & Carme Mangiron) - A specialized study covering the game localization pipeline, asset management, humor adaptation, string length limitations, and culturalization.

Advanced Interpreting

This track cultivates professional competence in simultaneous booth technique, conference preparation, and community interpreting in high-stakes public settings.

Conference Interpreting Pedagogy: Conference Interpreting: A Complete Course (Robin Setton & Andrew Dawrant) - An advanced training coursebook covering simultaneous interpreting mechanics, cognitive load distribution, decalage management, and international conference settings.

Conference Interpreting Virtual Practice: SCICtrain: Training Modules for Interpreting Students (European Commission DG Interpretation) - A free video training library produced by EU staff interpreters, providing demonstrations and exercises in simultaneous technique, retour interpreting, and accreditation preparation.

Community and Dialogue Interpreting: Community Interpreting (Sandra Hale) - A comprehensive academic and applied treatment of dialogue interpreting across courtroom, police, and healthcare environments, focusing on power dynamics and discursive roles.

Humanitarian and Public Service Practice: Interpreter Training Course (Kaya Connect / Save the Children) - A free forty-hour interactive training program providing scenarios, role-plays, and protocols for interpreting in humanitarian aid and community crisis encounters.

Translation Technology and MT Post-Editing

This track explores the computational systems driving modern translation, including neural machine translation architectures, corpus exploitation, and automated quality control.

Neural Machine Translation Architectures: Machine Translation (Coursera/Karlsruhe Institute of Technology) - A university-level open course covering the mathematical and algorithmic progression from statistical models to sequence-to-sequence neural networks, attention mechanisms, and BLEU evaluation.

Post-Editing Methodology and Quality Standards: MT Post-editing Guidelines (TAUS) - The global industry reference document defining light versus full post-editing criteria, throughput expectations, and error typologies.

Localization Engineering and Management: Introduction to Localization (Localization Academy) - A structured industry course examining the software localization life cycle, internationalization principles, translation management systems (TMS), and file parsing.

Corpus-Based Translation and Terminology Extraction: New Directions in Corpus-Based Translation Studies edited by Claudio Fantinuoli and Federico Zanettin (Language Science Press) - An open-access research volume exploring the compilation, tagging, and computational analysis of bilingual parallel and comparable corpora for translation practice.
