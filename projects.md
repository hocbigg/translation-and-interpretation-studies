## Create your own subtitle files

- [TED Translators: Get Started](https://www.ted.com/participate/translate/get-started) - The official onboarding guide for volunteer subtitlers, detailing transcription, interlingual subtitling, and review workflows on CaptionHub and Amara.
- [Quickstart Guide to Amara Subtitle Editor (US National Archives)](https://www.archives.gov/files/amara-quickstart-guide.pdf) - A practical step-by-step walkthrough covering subtitle entry, timecode synchronization, line breaks, and video caption export.
- [BBC Subtitle Guidelines](https://bbc.github.io/subtitle-guidelines/) - The comprehensive technical specification defining reading speeds, line lengths, display durations, and syntactic segmentation.
- [Netflix Timed Text Style Guide: General Requirements](https://partnerhelp.netflixstudios.com/hc/en-us/articles/215758617-Timed-Text-Style-Guide-General-Requirements) - The commercial industry benchmark specifying character limits, shot-change rules, dual-speaker dashes, and dialogue compression.

## Translate your own Wikipedia articles

- [Wikipedia: Content Translation Tool Guide](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wikipedia:Content_translation_tool) - The official Wikimedia manual on using the machine-assisted Content Translation environment to adapt cross-lingual wiki entries with synchronized references and infoboxes.
- [How to Translate a Wikipedia Article (wikiHow)](https://www.wikihow.com/Translate-a-Wikipedia-Article) - A visual walkthrough detailing source article selection, account configuration, target wiki formatting, and manual post-editing.
- [Wikipedia: Translation Guidelines and Best Practices](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wikipedia:Translation) - The community-consensus policy outlining attribution requirements, licensing compliance, neutral point of view preservation, and machine translation post-editing standards.

## Produce your own humanitarian translations

- [A Beginner's Guide to Translating Global Voices Stories (Global Voices)](https://community.globalvoices.org/2015/05/a-beginners-guide-to-translating-global-voices-stories/) - A step-by-step editorial guide for translating citizen journalism articles, focusing on journalistic style, local context glossing, and editorial review.
- [Translators without Borders Volunteer Translator Onboarding (CLEAR Global)](https://translatorswithoutborders.org/volunteer) - The orientation guide and platform portal for translating vital crisis relief information, healthcare instructions, and emergency communications for NGOs.
- [Kiva Review and Translation Team Guide](https://www.kiva.org/teams/review_and_translation) - A structured procedural guide for translating and editing real microloan borrower profiles from global field partners into clear, compliant English.

## Build your own terminology databases

- [How to Build a Termbase From Scratch (TCLoc Master's / University of Strasbourg)](https://mastertcloc.unistra.fr/how-to-build-a-termbase-from-scratch/) - A practitioner guide to designing concept-oriented termbases, establishing granular data categories, setting entry statuses, and adhering to ISO standards.
- [Terminology Management for Translation: A Starter Guide (COTSOES)](https://www.cotsoes.org/sites/default/files/CST_Terminology_Management_for_Translation_A_Starter_Guide.pdf) - The official inter-institutional guide from the Conference of Translation Services of European States on compiling, structuring, and maintaining institutional multilingual termbases.
- [OmegaT Documentation and Glossary Tutorial (OmegaT)](https://omegat.org/documentation) - The official technical manual on generating, formatting, and integrating plain-text tab-delimited and TBX glossary files within an open-source CAT workflow.

## Execute your own software localization projects

- [Introduction to Localization (Localization Academy)](https://localizationacademy.com/introduction-to-localization/) - A structured open course with hands-on assignments guiding learners through auditing a live product's localization readiness, managing file formats, and executing translation workflows.
- [Software Translation Workflows (Weblate Documentation)](https://docs.weblate.org/en/latest/workflows.html) - A comprehensive open documentation guide explaining string extraction, internationalization variable handling, plural forms, and continuous localization pipelines.

## Build your own translator portfolios

- [How to Create a Localization Portfolio (POEditor Blog)](https://poeditor.com/blog/localization-portfolio/) - A strategic walkthrough for curating translation and localization samples, drafting problem-solution case studies, and presenting UI adaptations without breaching non-disclosure agreements.
- [Getting Started in Game Localization: Building a Portfolio (Loc'd and Loaded)](https://locdandloaded.net/2023/05/09/getting-started-in-game-localization/) - An expert game localizer's detailed guide to assembling transcreation samples, mock game tests, and specialized glossaries to demonstrate subject-matter expertise.

## Write your own comparative translation analyses

- [How to Write a Comparative Analysis (Harvard College Writing Center)](https://writingcenter.fas.harvard.edu/pages/how-write-comparative-analysis) - The classic methodological guide on framing points of comparison, structuring thesis arguments, and alternating between text-by-text and point-by-point organizational schemes.
- [Tips for Reviewers: Comparative Analysis (Reading in Translation)](https://readingintranslation.com/2013/11/tips-for-reviewers-comparative-analysis/) - An applied essay demonstrating how to systematically compare divergent translations of the same source passage, highlighting prosody, lexical shifts, and translator stance.

## Write your own translation commentaries

- [Writing a Translation Commentary by Penélope Johnson (Google Books)](https://books.google.com/books?isbn=9781032227030) - [Paid book] The definitive pedagogical guide dedicated to drafting reflective academic commentaries, covering theoretical contextualization, skopos justification, and translation problem categorization.
- [Tips for Reviewers: Translators' Introductions Are Your Cheatsheet (Reading in Translation)](https://readingintranslation.com/2013/11/tips-for-reviewers-translators-introductions-are-your-cheatsheet/) - An analytical guide examining how published translators explain their theoretical strategies, historical constraints, and stylistic decisions in formal introductions.

## Conduct your own translation quality assessments

- [Multidimensional Quality Metrics (MQM) Definition and Error Typology (W3C)](https://themqm.org/) - The open, international framework defining standardized hierarchical categories and severity levels (minor, major, critical) for measuring translation and post-editing quality.
- [MT Post-editing Guidelines and Quality Benchmarks (TAUS)](https://www.taus.net/resources/reports/mt-post-editing-guidelines) - The industry reference defining error evaluation criteria, difference thresholds between light and full post-editing, and measurable translation quality metrics.

## Compile your own conference interpreter dossiers

- [Practical Guide for Professional Conference Interpreters: Preparing for the Conference (AIIC)](https://aiic.org/document/547/AIICWebzine_Apr2004_2_Practical_guide_for_professional_conference_interpreters_EN.pdf) - The official AIIC practitioner handbook on gathering source documentation, structuring domain briefings, building ad-hoc meeting glossaries, and aligning with organizers.
- [Speech Repository: Search Speeches (European Commission)](https://speech-repository.webcloud.ec.europa.eu/) - An extensive searchable database of over 4,000 real conference and training speeches organized by language, subject domain, and difficulty to prepare thematic mock dossiers against.
- [Online Resources for Conference Interpreter Training (ORCIT)](https://orcit.eu/) - An interactive training platform offering pedagogical modules on conference preparation, background research, and terminology activation.

## Record your own consecutive interpreting performances

- [SCICtrain: Training Modules for Interpreting Students (European Commission DG Interpretation)](https://commission.europa.eu/about/departments-and-executive-agencies/interpretation/interpretation-training-toolbox/scictrain-training-modules-interpreting-students_en) - A comprehensive open video training curriculum demonstrating consecutive interpreting techniques, active listening, split attention, and self-evaluation protocols.
- [Note-taking for Consecutive Interpreting: A Short Course by Andrew Gillies (Google Books)](https://books.google.com/books?isbn=9781138123205) - [Paid book] The standard practical coursebook on Rozan-style vertical notation, abbreviation systems, margin utilization, and self-auditing recorded consecutive deliveries.
